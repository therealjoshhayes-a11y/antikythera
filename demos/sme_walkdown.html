    Decision logic and limits

    Unit of review: one target course SLO, evaluated against a ranked list of distinct external training modules, one at a time, by one SME.

    Positive exit: stop immediately when the third distinct module is marked ACCEPT. This means three candidates were identified for further consideration—not that the SLO has automatic approved coverage.

    Negative exit: stop immediately after four REJECT decisions in a row. An ACCEPT resets the rejection streak to zero. This means the review's search budget was exhausted—not that all lower-ranked modules fail.

    Queue exhaustion: stop if the ranked candidate list ends first.

    Human authority: the algorithm provides a rank and informational similarity score, but no automatic Accept/Reject decisions. No score cutoff or combined similarity score decides an outcome.

    Governance: This reconstructed single-SME screen demonstrates the 3-accept / 4-consecutive-reject heuristic. It is distinct from the repository's separately documented three-SME MinK v3.2 protocol. Neither interface by itself confers credit or a formal institutional equivalency.
